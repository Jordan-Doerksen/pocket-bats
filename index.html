Bats are
wonderful.

Most people meet them as a shape in the dark. Up close they are soft, careful animals that hold the night together.

Three things people get wrong ↓

Three things people get wrong

They are blind.

No bat species is blind. All of them can see. Many fruit bats see very well in low light, and some species see ultraviolet.

They are flying mice.

They are not rodents. Bats are the only mammals that fly under their own power. Everything else that glides is falling with style.

They want your hair.

A bat near your head is hunting the insects near your head. It knows exactly where you are, which is the whole point of the next part.

See how it knows ↓

See with sound

A bat calls, then listens for the echo. From the echo it builds a picture of the dark: what is there, how far, and which way it is moving. It does this fast enough to catch a moth mid-turn.

Echo ping. Tap or press space to call. Tap a moth to catch it. Everything below still works if this does not.

What they do all night

1,400+

Known bat species. That is more than one in five mammal species on Earth.

Hundreds

Insects a small insect-eating bat can take in one night, mosquitoes included. A colony works through the number your imagination refuses to hold.

Upside down

Their foot tendons lock closed under their own weight. Hanging costs a bat no effort. Letting go is the part that takes a muscle.

Bananas

Also mangoes, guavas, and agave. Nectar bats pollinate over 500 plant species. Tequila exists because of a bat.

Two

Pups a year for many species, usually one. Bats are slow to replace their losses, which is why a lost colony stays lost.
